Welcome!

📢 Key message:

Our core collection-based pipeline unveils distinctive selection patterns between vegetable and grain soybeans, identifying selection footprints and favorable alleles in vegetable soybeans, and guiding genomic insights for enhanced breeding strategies.

📍 Keywords:

Vegetable soybean, population structure, diversity and differentiation, genomic scans, core collection, selection footprint.

⚠️NOTE: Some data in this book is unpublished; therefore, certain results may be uncompleted, simulated, and some plots may appear blurred.
